APEX MOTORS - MEMBER 5 (BEGINNER-FRIENDLY VERSION)

FILES:
1. about.html
2. contact.html
3. faq.html
4. style.css
5. script.js

HOW TO USE:
1. Download and extract this ZIP file.
2. Copy all five files into the same folder as your existing index.html.
3. Open about.html in your browser to see the About Us page.
4. Open contact.html to see the Contact Us page.
5. Open faq.html to see the Help & FAQ page.

CONNECT THESE PAGES FROM YOUR EXISTING HOMEPAGE:
About link:   href="about.html"
Contact link: href="contact.html"
Help & FAQ:   href="faq.html"

IMPORTANT:
- Update the sample phone number and email in contact.html with your group's approved details.
- The contact form currently shows a demo confirmation in the browser. It does not send an email or save a message to a database. A backend is needed for real submissions.
- The page photos load from Unsplash, so an internet connection is needed to display them.
- Existing homepage menu names and section IDs may differ. Adjust links if needed.
