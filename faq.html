01. HOW DO I BOOK A PRIVATE TEST DRIVE?

Click "Book a Test Drive" in the top navigation bar or visit our Test Drive page, select your preferred model, date, and showroom slot, and our concierge team will confirm your appointment within 2 hours.

02. ARE ALL VEHICLES CERTIFIED AND INSPECTED?

Yes. Every vehicle in the Car Vault collection undergoes a rigorous 180-point mechanical, structural, and provenance inspection before being listed in our showroom.

03. WHAT FINANCING AND LEASING OPTIONS ARE AVAILABLE?

We offer tailored luxury financing plans with flexible down payments, competitive rates, and custom tenure options. Visit our Finance page to estimate your monthly plan.

04. CAN I COMPARE MULTIPLE MODELS BEFORE VISITING?

Yes. Use the Compare Cars tool to view side-by-side performance specs, pricing, and features, or save your favorite vehicles to your Wishlist for quick access.

05. DO YOU ACCEPT TRADE-INS OR CONSIGNMENTS?

We accept select luxury and exotic vehicles for trade-in valuation or private consignment. Contact our Islamabad showroom team to schedule an appraisal.

06. WHAT ARE YOUR SHOWROOM HOURS AND LOCATION?

Our flagship showroom in Islamabad, Pakistan is open Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Private after-hours viewings can also be arranged on request.
